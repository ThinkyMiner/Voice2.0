## Notes

- Every statistic and decision comes from `analysis.analyze_look`, `boundaries.compute_boundaries` and `rules.evaluate_look`; this module only draws leads, injects the effect and counts outcomes.
- Effect injection follows PLAN §2.5 at lead level; a flipped lead's secondary is redrawn from pool leads with its new primary value.
- Runs stop at the first non-CONTINUE verdict, as the runner will. The naive path is followed separately to its own first decision on the same looks.
