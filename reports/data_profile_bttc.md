# Data profile: bttc

Experiment `vani-meeting-v1`: primary `meeting_fixed` = any answered call with disposition in ['Meeting Fixed']; window 14 days. Generated by `make profile DATASET=bttc` from the full data (deterministic, no sampling).

**Definitions.** Trigger = a lead's first answered call (§2.6). Baseline p at horizon H = share of triggered leads with an answered call in the primary set between the trigger and trigger + H days (inclusive of the trigger call). Censoring: the denominator for H holds only leads triggered at or before (last ts − H days), so every counted lead had full follow-up.

Unit evidence: the vendor reports back after the call ends, so the duration must fit in the gap `vendor_response_time − call_start_time` (seconds). Over 269,455 answered calls:

| unit hypothesis | seconds per unit | share of calls with duration ≤ gap | implied median answered call (s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| milliseconds | 0.001 | 1 | 0.022 |
| seconds | 1 | 1 | 22 |
| minutes | 60 | 0.1568 | 1,320 |

Lower envelope of gap ÷ duration (calls with duration > 0); a value just above 1 means the vendor reports right after the call ends:

| percentile | gap ÷ duration |
|---|---|
| p0.1 | 1.099 |
| p1 | 1.262 |
| p5 | 1.944 |
| p50 | 11.85 |

**Verdict: `duration_s` is in seconds**: the largest unit under which most answered calls (99.9996%, all but 1) fit inside the gap. Smaller units fit trivially but imply implausibly short calls; larger ones put most calls beyond the gap.
